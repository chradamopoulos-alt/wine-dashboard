# The guest booklet — what the 2025 edition gets wrong

Eight printed pages handed to guests at the tasting, written at roughly WSET
Level 1–2. Reproduced in the **Tasting 2027** tab as
`TASTING_2027.booklet`; proposed additions live in `.booklet.additions`,
rendered separately so the printed material and the drafts never read as one
thing.

Reviewed by the `sommelier` agent against the wines actually poured in
[[wine-tasting-2027]].

## The grape mismatch

The booklet profiles **nine grapes**: Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Pinot Noir,
Syrah/Shiraz, Grenache, Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc, Riesling, Assyrtiko.

All nine are poured, so none is wasted. The problem runs the other way: the five
concepts pour roughly **forty** named varieties. A guest will taste Carmenère,
Malbec, Zinfandel, Chenin Blanc, Glera, Garganega, Macabeo, Gamay, Vermentino,
Moschofilero, Xinomavro, Malagousia and more, and find none of them in the
booklet.

Worth a short profile, in priority order: Tempranillo, Malbec, Zinfandel,
Carmenère, Sangiovese, Chenin Blanc, Glera, Gamay, Pinot Grigio, Sémillon, and
the Greek three — Agiorgitiko, Xinomavro, Malagousia. The Greek omissions are
the most conspicuous for a hotel on Kos.

**Named but never poured:** Nebbiolo, Barbera, Pinotage. Drop them or mark them
as background.

**Do not grow the booklet to forty profiles.** Keep about sixteen and add a
loose one-page *Today's flight* insert per concept. A guest attending twice in a
week otherwise gets the same booklet against a different line-up.

## Errors in the eight pages

- Alcohol is given twice with different ranges, 10–15% and 11–15%, and neither
  covers the Moscato d'Asti that is actually poured.
- *Skin (Flavonoids)* is the wrong heading — skins give tannin, colour and
  flavour.
- *Acidity (crisp to soft)* should read low to high.
- The heading *(WSET Level 2 Style)* implies an affiliation. Remove it.
- Numbered lists lose their first number: `• Harvest → 2. Crush`.
- Page 7 has a duplicated heading; New Zealand and Portugal are missing.
- Page 8 gives Sauvignon Blanc as Loire, Marlborough, California. The tasting
  pours Bordeaux, Rueda, Chile and the Cape.
- Spelling mixes US and British forms.
- Kos is not mentioned anywhere. Fixed — see the additions in the dashboard.

## The page 5 pairing table

It extracts garbled from the PDF, the two columns interleaved. The content
itself is sound. Reconstructed, it is seven rows:

| If the food is… | …the wine seems |
| --- | --- |
| Sweet | drier, more bitter |
| Umami | more bitter, less fruity |
| Salty | softer, fruitier |
| Acidic | less sharp, more fruity |
| Rich or fatty | needs acidity to cut through |
| Spicy (chilli) | alcohol burns hotter |
| Intense flavours | needs a bold enough wine |

Two weaknesses: the last three rows are advice rather than effect, so the column
is inconsistent; and it never says what to pour instead. **Check the printed
copy** — it is not known whether the garbling is an extraction artefact or a
real print defect.

## The biggest gap

Nothing on **how place shapes a wine** — cool against warm climate, why the same
grape tastes different elsewhere. That is the thesis of every concept, and
adding it would improve the session more than anything else on this list.

After that, in order: a usable tasting method with a fill-in card; reading a
label; serving temperatures and glassware; pairing as an experiment using the
nine bites; how sparkling and sweet wines are made; oak and malolactic; a short
*is it off?* box on cork taint and oxidation.

## Staff sheet, not guest booklet

Keep out of the guest booklet and put on a separate sheet: run-of-show timings,
glassware and logistics, per-wine talking points, the full pronunciation guide,
and the **allergen list for the nine bites** — nuts, fish, gluten, dairy, pork
and honey, which has to be asked about before the session starts.

On sulphites: a qualitative answer only. No figures, no health claims.

## Caveats on this review

The sommelier inferred grapes from wine names and appellations because
`TASTING_2027` carries no grape field; cross-check against `STOCK_DATA` before
printing. It also could not see the images — cover, aroma wheel, colour chart,
world map are all image-only pages.
